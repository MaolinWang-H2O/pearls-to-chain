# 散珠串成链 · pearls-to-chain

> Don't let your stray thoughts disappear — leave them with us, and we'll quietly string them into a bracelet you'll be proud to wear.
>
> 别丢掉你随口的碎碎念，交给我们，在后台悄悄帮你串成能戴出门的手链。

**pearls-to-chain** is a human-AI collaboration pipeline that turns your scattered fragments — voice notes, rants, brainstorms — through five workstations into something shippable, reusable, and deliverable (a long-form article / a proposal / a Skill), instead of a pile of notes you never open again.

**散珠串成链** 是一套「人机协作打磨流水线」——把你随手丢过来的碎片想法、语音吐槽、头脑风暴，经过五道工序，打磨成**能分发、能复用、能交付**的成品（一篇长文 / 一份方案 / 一个 Skill），而不是一堆存下来却再也没打开过的笔记。

---

## Why you need it · 为什么需要它

The real value isn't the data — it's the process of turning raw material into a finished work: how conflicts arise, how blockers break, how consensus forms. This tool turns every fragmented exchange into a living, self-running knowledge base: **the process is the asset; the work is just its byproduct.**

数据只是数字和画面，真正值钱的是把杂乱原材料打磨成作品的过程——冲突怎么发生、卡点怎么破、共识怎么达成。本工具把每一次碎片化交流，沉淀成**活的、自运转的知识库**：过程即资产，作品只是过程的副产品。

Most people's pain: too many ideas, too many notes, nothing finished. Most tools stop at "save it" (putting loose beads in a box — still loose). pearls-to-chain makes the final leap: **de-water, shape, ship.**

普通人的痛点：想法很多、记了一堆、最后什么都没成型。多数工具止步于"存下来"（把散珠装进盒子，仍是散珠）。散珠串成链完成最后一跃——**去水、定型、出成品**。

## 30-second quick start · 30 秒怎么用

1. Start a new chat and dump your stray thoughts in (voice-to-text, rants, broken sentences — **don't tidy up**).
2. Say: "help me string it together".
3. The pipeline runs; you get a "bracelet" — structured, de-watered, ready to use.

开一个新对话，直接把你的碎碎念丢进来（语音转文字、吐槽、断句都行，**别整理**）。说一句："帮我串一下"。流水线跑完，你会拿到一串"手链"——结构化、去过水、能直接用的成品。

You don't need to "think it through first". Daring to speak beats speaking clearly.

不需要你"想清楚再说"。你敢开口，就大于你说清楚。

## What it does NOT do · 它不做什么（边界）

- 🔵 **No decisions for you** — the final call, the ship, the choice, is yours.
  **不替你拍板**：最后取舍、发货、做决定，都是你的事。
- 🔵 **No flattery as fact** — the AI's pretty summaries get "evaporated" before responding.
  **不把外脑奉承当事实**：AI 外脑的漂亮总结会先"蒸发"再回应。
- 🔵 **No groundless facts** — any "you said / did / felt" claim is reconciled with you first.
  **不进无据事实**：凡带"你说过/做过/感动过"的陈述，先跟你对账。
- 🔵 **No feature bloat** — it grows strong by knowing what NOT to do, not by adding power.
  **不堆功能**：它变强靠"知道什么不做"，不靠加能力。
- 🔵 **Your words, not invented ones** — feedback in plain language; follows your own phrasing, invents no new concepts for you.
  **用你的词、不替你创造**：反馈是大白话；顺着你自己的描述走，不替你发明新概念。

## The five-workstation pipeline · 五工位流水线

```
[fragment/voice/rant] → ① Catch → ② Reconcile → ③ Refine → ④ Land → ⑤ Rewrite
                          ↑                                    │
                          └──── each round feeds the living DB ──┘
```

| Station | Does | You feel |
|---|---|---|
| ① Catch | no interruption, grab 3 core words | "you go, I catch" |
| ② Reconcile | confirm "what you said"; external-verify strong claims | fabrications flagged red |
| ③ Refine | evaporate water → check false causality → red-team | get the substance |
| ④ Land | build skeleton, quantify anchors | directly consumable structure |
| ⑤ Rewrite | deposit process assets into living DB | smoother next time |

① 接住碎片 → ② 对账核实 → ③ 提纯打磨 → ④ 落盘成型 → ⑤ 经验回写
